Privacy notice

What PermitPulse collects and how it is used.

Effective June 3, 2026. PermitPulse is a small B2B service for home-service businesses. This notice covers the PermitPulse website, sales inquiries, pilot customers, and related sample materials.

Information PermitPulse collects

  • Identifiers and contact details, such as name, company name, email address, phone number, mailing details if provided, and message contents.
  • Commercial or business information shared during pilot discussions, such as service area, trade, delivery preferences, and account or billing context.
  • Internet or network activity information made available through routine site operation, such as IP address, browser type, device metadata, referring pages, and request timing.
  • Public building-permit records and related public-government data obtained from official sources.

Sources of information

  • Directly from you when you email, reply to outreach, request samples, or discuss a pilot.
  • Automatically from hosting, infrastructure, and email systems used to operate the site and service.
  • From official public-government sources that publish permit or related building-record information.

How PermitPulse uses information

  • To answer inquiries and provide requested samples.
  • To evaluate, set up, deliver, and support a pilot or subscription.
  • To maintain site security, troubleshoot problems, and keep basic records of communications.
  • To send commercial outreach or follow-up messages in connection with PermitPulse, subject to applicable law and opt-out rights.

What PermitPulse does not do

  • It does not sell your personal information.
  • It does not claim ownership of public government records.
  • It does not promise that source data is complete, real-time, or error-free.
  • It does not run consumer accounts, member dashboards, or behavioral ad targeting on this website.

How information may be shared

PermitPulse may share information with service providers used to run the business, such as website hosting, email, payment, document, or analytics vendors, but only as reasonably necessary to operate the service. Information may also be disclosed to comply with law, enforce agreements, or protect rights and safety.

Security

PermitPulse uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information it keeps. No system can promise absolute security, but PermitPulse aims to collect less data, limit access, and retain information only as long as reasonably needed for business and legal purposes.

Retention

Inquiry emails, pilot records, and customer communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable law to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to ask questions about PermitPulse's data practices. PermitPulse will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law. If PermitPulse sends you marketing email, you may opt out by replying with an unsubscribe request or using any unsubscribe method provided in the message. Service-related communications about an active customer relationship may still be sent when necessary to deliver the service.

Do Not Track and tracking technologies

This site does not currently respond to browser Do Not Track signals. PermitPulse does not knowingly use cross-site advertising cookies on this site, but hosting and infrastructure providers may process routine technical data needed to serve the site.

Public data and downstream use

PermitPulse uses public building-permit records from government sources. Those records can include delays, omissions, and source-level errors. A permit record does not necessarily establish that work was performed, is current, or is a suitable sales target. Customers are responsible for their own downstream marketing, outreach, and legal compliance when using any PermitPulse output.

Children

PermitPulse is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

PermitPulse may update this notice from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised when material updates are posted.
