PermitPulse is a small B2B service. This page explains what information is collected, what is not collected, and how contact requests are handled.

What PermitPulse Collects

  • Information you send directly by email
  • Basic website analytics or hosting logs provided by Vercel
  • Public building-permit records from official local government sources

What PermitPulse Does Not Claim

  • It does not claim ownership of government data
  • It does not promise complete, real-time, or error-free records
  • It does not sell a guarantee of jobs or closed revenue

Use of Public Data

PermitPulse uses public building-permit data for business monitoring and alerting. Public records may still require responsible use, manual review, and compliance with applicable law and source terms.

Contact Requests

If you email PermitPulse, your message and contact details may be retained to answer your inquiry, provide samples, or manage a pilot account. You can request that future marketing contact stop at any time.
