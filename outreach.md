## Objection Handling

`We already buy leads.`

This is not replacing your lead source. It gives your team fresh permit intelligence from the city each morning so they can move faster on active roofing demand.

`Are these exclusive?`

No. This is a speed and visibility product, not a fake exclusivity pitch.

`Do you have live examples?`

Yes. Send the sample alert in `sample-alert.md`.

`How current is it?`

The digest is built from public City of Chicago permit data and should be treated as a monitoring input, not a guarantee of immediate buying intent.

`Why would I pay for this instead of checking permits myself?`

The value is the packaging: one narrow feed, manual review notes, and a short weekday digest that your team can act on quickly.
