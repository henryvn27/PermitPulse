What is included

  • Recent Chicago roof-replacement permits from public city data
  • Address, permit number, issue date, and work summary
  • Owner and applicant names when present in the record
  • Short manual notes to help sort the feed faster

Who this is for

  • Roofing companies already doing outbound sales
  • Owners who want a narrow daily signal, not another platform
  • Teams willing to review sample data before buying

How the service works

  1. Public City of Chicago permit records are monitored.
  2. Roof-related entries are filtered into a Chicago roofing feed.
  3. A short weekday digest is sent for review by the buyer's team.

What this is not

  • Not an exclusivity claim
  • Not a guarantee of closed jobs
  • Not a city-affiliated service
  • Not a claim that every permit is high intent
